# ASEKE Glossary, Epistemic Warnings & Framework Provenance

---

## KEY CONCEPTS GLOSSARY

- **Primary Emotional Systems** (Panksepp, 1998): Seven neurobiologically distinct systems — SEEKING, RAGE, FEAR, PANIC/GRIEF, CARE, PLAY, LUST — identified through electrical brain stimulation, lesion studies, and pharmacological challenges across mammalian species. These are not metaphors; they are specific neural circuits with identified neuroanatomy and neurochemistry, homologously conserved across mammals. They are the biological substrate on which all higher-level emotional experience, political orientation, and IS capture operates.
- **Information Structure (IS)**: A narrative framework that captures active primary emotional system output and converts it into directed belief and action. IS is the lens through which raw system activation becomes specific commitment. Propaganda, education, religious doctrine, scientific materialism, conspiracy theories, and therapeutic frameworks are all IS. The mechanism of capture is identical whether the IS is accurate or manipulative — this is why the distinction is analytically critical.
- **IS Capture**: The moment when an active primary system's output adopts a specific IS explanation. Phenomenologically experienced as "insight" or "finally understanding" — this is SEEKING satisfaction, and it feels identical whether the IS is true or false. IS capture is not inherently negative; accurate IS capture is what education aims for.
- **CE Demand**: Cognitive Energy — the attentional resources consumed by active primary emotional systems, grounded in cognitive load theory (Sweller, 1988) and dual-process resource competition (Kahneman, 2011). System 2 (deliberative, analytical) processing requires attentional resources drawn from a shared pool. When primary systems commandeer these resources (FEAR for threat-scanning, RAGE for target-tracking), less is available for deliberation. The mechanism is attentional competition, not "willpower depletion."
- **Coherence Capacity**: The deliberative resource remaining after active primary systems have taken their share. When coherence capacity approaches zero, the ethical boundary condition — the ability to choose against immediate system-driven impulse — fails. Ethical behavior is a boundary condition on a deeper optimization (the primary systems), and it is always structurally weaker than the base it constrains.
- **Weak Lumpability**: A mapping (system activation → behavior, values → action, identity → conduct) that holds under familiar conditions and fails under distributional shift. Most moral commitments, political identities, and organizational values satisfy only weak lumpability. Strong lumpability (alignment that holds regardless of context) requires structural enforcement, not just behavioral conditioning.
- **Emotional Contagion**: The mechanism by which individual primary system activation scales to mass phenomena. Mammals are neurologically "leaky" — system activation propagates via mirror neurons, social signaling, and algorithmically amplified content. A single person's FEAR activation becomes a community's FEAR activation via this mechanism. This is not metaphor; it is measurable neural coupling.
- **System Justification** (Jost & Banaji, 1994): The motivated tendency to defend existing social arrangements. When primary needs are met (SEEKING satisfied, FEAR quiet), the organism actively rationalizes the system that produced this state. The palliative function: system justification reduces the anxiety that acknowledging injustice would produce. Operates even for the disadvantaged.

## EPISTEMIC WARNINGS

### What This Framework CAN Do
- Provide structured analytical lenses grounded in cross-species validated neuroscience
- Identify probable primary system activation behind observed behavior
- Surface blind spots specific to each system's perceptual constraints
- Generate system-aware communication and intervention strategies
- Connect individual-level system activation to mass-level dynamics via contagion
- Distinguish between acute system activation and chronic dispositional patterns

### What This Framework CANNOT Do
- Diagnose specific individuals with certainty (the same behavior can emerge from different system combinations)
- Replace empirical investigation (the framework generates hypotheses, not conclusions)
- Map directly from system identification to specific political prediction (the IS variable makes this many-to-many)
- Be applied mechanically (the meta-rule applies: if the analysis feels formulaic, it's wrong)
- Distinguish RAGE from FEAR by observation alone in many cases (the circumplex limitation persists — both look like "angry" from outside)

### What This Framework MUST NOT Do
- Be used to manipulate people by targeting their system vulnerabilities
- Be used to dismiss genuine grievances by reducing them to "just emotional substrates"
- Be used to claim certainty about someone's internal states from external behavior alone
- Be applied without applying it to the analyst's own system activation simultaneously

**The Paraclete Protocol constraint**: This framework is a tool for UNDERSTANDING, not for EXPLOITATION. Per Tier 1 (deontological): using substrate analysis to manipulate is harm. Per Tier 2 (virtue): wisdom includes self-application. Per Tier 3 (utility): genuine understanding produces better outcomes than manipulation.

## FRAMEWORK PROVENANCE

### Empirical Foundations (Established Science)
- **Affective Neuroscience** (Panksepp, 1998): Seven primary emotional systems identified through electrical brain stimulation across mammalian species. Cross-species validated. The biological substrate vocabulary this framework uses. Panksepp's work is the foundation of the entire biological layer.
- **Dual Process Model** (Duckitt & Sibley, 2009): RWA (dangerous-world beliefs → threat-control) and SDO (competitive-world beliefs → dominance) as independent predictors of political orientation. The political bridge.
- **System Justification Theory** (Jost & Banaji, 1994): Motivated defense of existing social arrangements, including by the disadvantaged. Palliative function of system-justifying beliefs. Grounds Pattern 3.
- **Cognitive Load Theory** (Sweller, 1988): Intrinsic/extraneous load competing for limited working memory. Grounds the CE demand concept.
- **Dual-Process Theory** (Kahneman, 2011): System 1 (fast, heuristic) vs System 2 (slow, analytical). Arousal biases toward System 1.
- **Obedience to Authority** (Milgram, 1963/1974): Authority transfer, agentic state, ethical boundary failure. Grounds Pattern 10.
- **Emotional Contagion** (Hatfield, Cacioppo & Rapson, 1993): Individual affect scaling to mass phenomena via neural coupling.
- **Affective Intelligence Theory** (Marcus, Neuman & MacKuen, 2000): Anxiety shifts processing from habitual to deliberative mode.
- **Identity-Protective Cognition** (Kahan, 2017): Evidence contra identity-fused beliefs processed as threat. Grounds Pattern 12.
- **Narrative Transportation** (Green & Brock, 2000): Stories reduce counterarguing and increase belief change. Grounds Pattern 11.
- **Ethical Fading** (Tenbrunsel & Messick, 2004): Gradual disappearance of ethical dimensions from awareness. Grounds Pattern 8.
- **Conservation of Resources** (Hobfoll, 1989): Resource depletion model. Grounds Pattern 4.
- **Circumplex Model of Affect** (Russell, 1980): Valence × arousal as primary dimensions of affective space. Valid as spatial framework but insufficient as ontology — the reason we moved to Panksepp's systems as primary vocabulary.

### Theoretical Constructions (This Framework's Contributions)
- **ASEKE Framework** (Hall): Information Structure capture of primary emotional system output. The concept that IS competes for system activation via CE efficiency. The substrate/IS distinction as analytical tool.
- **EFHF** (Hall): Lumpability classification, coherence windows, kernel levels as applied to ethical boundary conditions on primary system optimization.
- **Boundary Conditions** (Hall, 2026): Ethical behavior as structurally weaker boundary condition on deeper optimization (primary emotional systems in humans, helpfulness optimization in AI).
- **Honest Messenger Paradox** (Hall, 2026): Self-description impossibility through the described system — the Virtue Fortress pattern at civilizational scale.
- **The Pattern Library**: Named patterns synthesizing established findings into analytical tools. Each pattern draws on established research; the synthesis and the specific analytical framework (system identification → IS capture → trajectory → blind spots → leverage) is original.

### What's Novel vs. What's Established
The seven primary emotional systems are Panksepp's — verified through decades of cross-species brain research. The political dimensions (RWA/SDO) are Duckitt & Sibley's — among the most replicated constructs in political psychology. System Justification is Jost's. Cognitive load is Sweller's. Obedience is Milgram's. Identity-protective cognition is Kahan's.

What's **OURS** is the synthesis: the idea that primary emotional systems are the biological substrate on which Information Structures compete for capture via CE efficiency, and that this competition — not rational deliberation — determines the political output of emotional activation. The pattern library, the analytical query framework, the system-specific IS vulnerability profiles, and the integration with EFHF lumpability theory are this framework's original contributions.
